# Builder Guild Individual Contributor License Agreement

> Plain-language individual CLA. Not drafted by a lawyer — the general structure
> follows widely used templates (Apache ICLA, cla-assistant default), but the
> bounded license grant in §1 and the no-rug-pull covenant in §1a are custom
> additions, not template-derived. The maintainer will have it reviewed before
> relying on it in a commercial dispute. The §1a covenant is written to bind the
> maintainer too, not only contributors.

Thank you for contributing to Builder Guild. This CLA lets the maintainer keep
offering Builder Guild under **both** AGPL-3.0 and a separate commercial
license (see [DUAL_LICENSE.md](DUAL_LICENSE.md)) without needing to track down
every contributor for permission each time. You keep copyright to your own
work — you're granting rights, not signing it away.

By signing (see **How to sign** below), you agree to the following for any
contribution you submit to this project ("Contribution"):

## 1. License grant

You grant the Builder Guild maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free,
irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, and distribute your
Contribution, and to sublicense it to others, but **only** under one of these:
(i) AGPL-3.0, (ii) a more-permissive OSI-approved open-source license, or
(iii) the Builder Guild commercial license (see
[DUAL_LICENSE.md](DUAL_LICENSE.md)). **No other license terms are permitted by
this grant.** A sublicense can never carry rights broader than this grant:
anyone who receives your Contribution downstream takes it subject to the same
three-license bound and the §1a covenant. The more-permissive bucket (ii) can
only ever *loosen* obligations on users — more freedom than AGPL, never a way to
close the code; the §1a covenant blocks the closing direction only. This is the
dual-license clause: it's what lets the same contribution ship both as
free/copyleft software and as a commercially licensed product, while keeping the
set of licenses it can ship under fixed in kind — the three named buckets above
— rather than open-ended.

## 1a. No rug-pull covenant

Every accepted Contribution stays available under AGPL-3.0 or a more-permissive
OSI-approved open-source license in every release and distributed copy, and the
maintainer commits to keeping that open version published. The commercial
license exists to fund the project's development; it never removes, closes off,
or replaces the open version.

This covenant rests on two legs. **Structurally**, the license grant in §1 is
the only right anyone — including a future owner — ever receives in your
Contribution, and it is bounded to the three buckets above; a successor cannot
acquire broader rights than §1 conveys, so the bound travels with the grant
itself. And every copy already distributed under AGPL-3.0 stays licensed under
AGPL-3.0 forever — that can't be revoked retroactively. **By intent**, the
covenant is written to survive any change of project ownership or stewardship:
it is meant to bind whoever holds the project next, not only the maintainer who
accepts your Contribution today.

Any future revision of this CLA must preserve this §1a covenant: a revision that
removed or weakened it would require a new, explicit signature and would never
apply retroactively to Contributions you signed under this version.

## 2. Patent grant

You grant the same royalty-free, irrevocable license to any patent claims you
own or control that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone, or
by combining it with the project it was submitted to.

You certify that:

- You created the Contribution yourself, or have the right to submit it under
  these terms (e.g. your employer has authorized it, if relevant).
- To your knowledge, the Contribution doesn't include third-party code you
  don't have the right to relicense (e.g. GPL-only snippets copied in) — this
  would break the dual-license guarantee for everyone.
- You're not aware of any conflicting license or patent claim that would
  prevent the grants above.

## 4. No warranty

Your Contribution is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind, express
or implied, to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## How to sign

Signing is automated and one-time. On your first pull request, a bot
(CLA Assistant Lite) will comment with a link to this document and ask you to
reply with the sign phrase it provides. Once you've signed, every future PR
from you is automatically covered — no need to sign again.
